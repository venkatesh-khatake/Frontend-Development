🥔🥦Aloo Gobi (Potato & Cauliflower Curry)

🛒 Ingredients:

  • 2 potatoes (cubed)
  • 1 medium cauliflower (cut into florets)
  • 1 tomato (chopped)
  • 1–2 green chilies (optional)
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • Salt (as per taste)
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Fresh coriander leaves (for garnish)

👨‍🍳 Steps:

  1. Heat oil in a pan.
  2. Add chopped onion and sauté until golden brown.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for 1 minute.
  4. Add tomatoes, turmeric, red chili powder, coriander powder, and salt. Cook until oil separates.
  5. Add potatoes and cauliflower. Mix well.
  6. Cover and cook on low flame for 15–20 minutes (stir occasionally).
  7. Once soft, add garam masala and mix.
  8. Garnish with fresh coriander.

🍽️ Serve with:

  • Roti
  • Paratha
  • Rice
